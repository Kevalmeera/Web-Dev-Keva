5 Facts About Cha Woo-min

Real Name

His real name is Kim Min-woo, but he is professionally known as Cha Woo-min.

Birthday

He was born on October 24, 2000, in Busan, South Korea.

Acting Career

He is a South Korean actor known for his roles in Weak Hero Class 1, Night Has Come, and Study Group.

Cat Lovers

Beyond his on-screen characters, Cha Woo-min is said to love cats. Imagine the contrast between his intense drama characters and adorable feline friends!

Poem Lovers

Cha Woo-min's most cherished book is a collection of poems by South Korean poet Jeong Ho-seung. This reveals his appreciation for poetry and the beauty of expressing emotions through words.
