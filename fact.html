5 FACTS ABOUT PSYCHOLOGY

The Human Psyches

Psychology is the scientific study of the human mind and behavior. It bridges the gap between brain function and outward actions, helping us understand why we think, feel, and act the way we do.

Neuroplasticity

The human brain is not hardwired; it is incredibly adaptable. Through a process called neuroplasticity, the brain constantly rewires itself and creates new neural pathways based on your daily experiences and habits.

Cognitive Dissonance

When you hold two conflicting beliefs, your brain experiences psychological discomfort. To ease this tension, humans will subconsciously change their attitude or find excuses to justify their behavior.

The Power of Groups

Human behavior is deeply shaped by the people around us. We have a natural urge to conform to group norms, which can sometimes cause individuals to make decisions they wouldn't normally make alone.

The 66-Day Rule

Research suggests it takes an average of 66 days for a new behavior to become completely automatic. Building a lasting habit relies heavily on consistency and setting up the right environmental cues.
